EV-LAB IMPROVEMENT REPLACEMENT FILES

Replace these files in your existing EV-LAB project, preserving all other files:

lib/main.dart
lib/theme/app_theme.dart
lib/screens/settings_screen.dart
lib/screens/home_screen.dart
lib/screens/virtual_lab_screen.dart
lib/screens/result_screen.dart
lib/screens/structure_screen.dart
assets/models/catalase.glb
assets/models/lipase.glb

After replacing:
1. Run: flutter pub get
2. Run: flutter analyze
3. Run: flutter run

The package does NOT include the rest of the project, so your existing database,
lesson data, dependencies, web folder, Android/iOS folders, and other screens are
left untouched.
