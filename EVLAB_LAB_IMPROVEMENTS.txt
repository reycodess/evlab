EV-LAB LAB IMPROVEMENTS

This build is based on the uploaded EV-LAB project and keeps the existing VirtualLabScreen architecture rather than replacing it with a shortened screen.

Interactive laboratory improvements:
- Restored the realistic VirtualThermometer and RealisticPHControl widgets in the main laboratory.
- Added explicit sliders directly beneath both realistic instruments so temperature and pH can always be changed reliably.
- Instrument displays update immediately when the sliders move.
- Live predicted activity updates while temperature, pH, substrate, mixing, and inhibitor ratio are changed.
- Temperature and pH controls are enabled after their corresponding bench calibration step, preventing the old stuck-control problem while retaining preparation realism.
- Added Explore Conditions presets for cold, optimum, heat stress, acidic, and basic conditions.
- Added optional inhibitor preparation to the visible Bench Setup instead of relying on the old hidden equipment drawer.
- Preserved the existing Michaelis-Menten and inhibition calculations, missions, XP, history, analysis, reaction chamber, and molecular animation.

3D model improvements:
- Catalase and Lipase GLB files were re-exported with explicit PBR materials and vertex colors so they render as colored protein models.
- Amylase remains included.
- The viewer keeps camera controls and a molecular-animation fallback while the real GLB loads.

After replacing the project files:
1. flutter clean
2. flutter pub get
3. flutter run -d emulator-5554

Note: the protein colors are educational visualization colors, not atom-by-atom chemical coloring. The kinetic simulation remains a model rather than a physical laboratory measurement.
